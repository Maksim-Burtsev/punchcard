# Logo generation prompts

Style must pair with Ponytail's logo: rough hand-drawn black ink sketch on
white, careless doodle linework, ink splatter and stray dots, no color.

## Avatar / logo (`assets/logo.png`)

> Rough hand-drawn black ink sketch on white background, careless doodle
> style with ink splatters and stray dots. Portrait of an elderly master
> programmer: large balding head, gray hair in loose strokes at the sides,
> full thick beard drawn in sweeping strokes, bushy eyebrows, two or three
> deep forehead wrinkles. Small half-moon bifocal glasses slid down to the
> tip of his nose, eyes looking OVER the glasses straight at the viewer —
> the tired, seen-it-all stare of a man who saw your mistake back in 1987.
> Slightly unimpressed frown. Suspender straps hinted at the shoulders, the
> corner of a punch card sticking out of his shirt pocket. Monochrome ink
> only, no background details.

## Social preview (`assets/social-preview.png`)

> Same character and ink style, half-length at a desk: a stack of thick worn
