# Logo generation prompts

Style must pair with Ponytail's logo: rough hand-drawn black ink sketch on
white, careless doodle linework, ink splatter and stray dots, no color.

## Avatar / logo (`assets/logo.png`)

> Rough hand-drawn black ink sketch on white background, careless doodle
> style with ink splatters and stray dots. Portrait of an elderly master
> programmer: large balding head, gray hair in loose strokes at the sides,
> full thick beard drawn in sweeping strokes, bushy eyebrows, two or three
> deep forehead wrinkles. Small half-moon bifocal glasses slid down to the
> tip of his nose, eyes looking OVER the glasses straight at the viewer —
> the tired, seen-it-all stare of a man who saw your mistake back in 1987.
> Slightly unimpressed frown. Suspender straps hinted at the shoulders, the
> corner of a punch card sticking out of his shirt pocket. Monochrome ink
> only, no background details.

## Social preview (`assets/social-preview.png`)

> Same character and ink style, half-length at a desk: a stack of thick worn
> hardcover books beside him (no readable titles on the spines), one hand on
> a coffee mug, a punch card used as a bookmark in the top book. Wide
> composition, monochrome ink on white.

## Banner (`assets/banner.png`) — shipped

The version in the repository. Two corrections over the earlier drafts: a
full head of hair (every bald variant read as a caricature), and the
tagline hand-lettered with the same pen as the title — a typeface under
hand-drawn capitals broke the sketch.

> Wide horizontal composition, rough hand-drawn black ink sketch on white,
> careless doodle style with ink splatters, monochrome only. Elderly
> programmer, late 60s: a full head of thick messy gray hair swept back,
> Einstein-ish volume at the sides, a few loose strands (not bald, no
> receding hairline). Thick gray beard, half-moon glasses low on the tip of
> his nose, warm kind amused eyes. Shirt with suspenders, a punch card in
> the shirt pocket. Half-length at a desk, slightly left of center: a stack
> of thick worn hardcover books beside him (no readable titles), one hand
> resting on a coffee mug, a punch card used as a bookmark in the top book.
> On the right, large hand-lettered "PUNCHCARD" in rough ink capitals, and
> below it in smaller handwriting drawn with the same pen and the same
> wobbly, uneven strokes — clearly the same hand, not a typeface —
> "Reviewing code since it came on cardboard." Plenty of white space, no
> other text, no frame, no border.
